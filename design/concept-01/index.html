02 · Three interface states

A simpler optical response

  1. 01

    No contact

    Microscopic air gaps diffusely reflect light, producing a light-gray tactile image.

  2. 02

    Local contact

    Pressure increases membrane–gel optical coupling and reduces reflection locally.

  3. 03

    Increasing pressure

    A larger fraction of the interface couples, so the response becomes larger and darker.

# GlowTact Website Asset Manifest

## Rules
- Prefer original high-resolution exports over PDF screenshots.
- Preserve axes, labels, colors, and scientific meaning.
- Keep GlowTact amber and GelSight Mini blue.
- Use SVG for diagrams when practical.
- Use WebP/AVIF/PNG for images.
- Do not generate fake experimental images or data.

### 3D reconstruction turntables

The reconstruction section shows GlowTact output only — seven pressed objects,
each as a rotating reconstruction with the tactile frame it was computed from.
The source gallery is regenerated by `scripts/glowtact/spin_gallery.py` in the
FoundationTactile repository from `config/figures/glowtact_recon_gallery.json`,
and lands in `~/tactile_data/glowtact/analysis/3d_recon_pad/gallery/`. Every
object is rendered with the accepted figure run's parameters
(`figure_angles/three_objects_improved/render_params.json`): the 3x3
reconstruction, 3 px mesh and shading smoothing, z_scale 1, the steep figure
angle `front [0, 0.85, 0.53]`, and zoom 0.62. The spec file records the two
deliberate departures — the contact stays centred at 0.5/0.5 because an
off-centre relief swings through an arc on a turntable, and the render canvas
is 1160 px rather than 900 to hold the output resolution at the lower zoom.

| Review asset | Source |
|---|---|
| `assets/video/recon/<object>-spin.mp4` | `<object>/<object>_spin.gif` |
| `assets/images/reconstruction/<object>-spin-poster.jpg` | first frame of the same GIF |
| `assets/images/reconstruction/<object>-tactile.jpg` | `<object>/<object>_tactile.png` |

Objects: `screw-threads`, `philips-head`, `cali-balls`, `oreo`, `swirls`,
`battery`, `hex-head`.

The turntables ship as H.264 rather than as the source GIFs. The seven GIFs are
41 MB; the same 72 frames at 720 px wide are 2.7 MB as MP4, and re-encoding the
GIFs (12 fps, 96 colors) only made them larger, because dithering the speckled
mesh defeats LZW. Each `<video>` is muted, looping, `playsinline`, and carries
its first frame as a poster, so it reads as a GIF and still animates without JS.
Playback is gated on the reduced-motion preference, on visibility, and on the
section's own pause control.

Concept 03 uses `assets/images/hero-teaser.jpg` as its first-screen paper teaser.
The figure is displayed without cropping, and its original scientific
annotations are preserved.

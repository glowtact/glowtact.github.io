SYS–02 Mechanism states

The signal is the interface changing.

  1. 00

    Air gap

    Microscopic gaps diffusely reflect light at the unloaded interface.

  2. 01

    Partial coupling

    Pressure brings the black membrane into local optical contact.

  3. 02

    Expanded coupling

    A larger interface fraction couples and the tactile region darkens.

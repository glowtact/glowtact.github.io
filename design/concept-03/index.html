DAT / 01 Light touch

It sees two millinewtons.

An M&M at 1.0 g (9.8 mN) and an M2 screw at 0.2 g (1.96 mN) both show in the raw frame under their own weight.

Passive placement is a demonstration; the controlled threshold test gives a 0.12 N median minimum detectable force at SNR = 3 across 10 probes.

DAT / 02 Geometry

One dark image, turned in three dimensions.

Relief from a single frame’s darkening resolves M1 threads at 0.25 mm pitch.

TURNTABLE

Qualitative relief from one tactile frame, not validated against measured heights.

DAT / 03 Shear

It reads the sideways pull.

Dense Farnebäck flow on the gel’s own speckle yields a usable field over 97.8 % of the pad, 0.037 px forward–backward.

Readouts per frame: slide, twist, spread, slip; the red outline is the contact.

Darkness carries the load.
